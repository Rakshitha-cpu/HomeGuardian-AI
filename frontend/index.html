The problem

One inspection can't see five years ahead.

Buyers commit their life savings on a single, subjective walkthrough — and pay for what it missed, later, when it's more expensive to fix.

01 / STATIC

One-time snapshot

Inspections capture a single day. Deterioration, leaks, and wear keep happening after the report is filed.

02 / SUBJECTIVE

Inspector-dependent

Findings vary by who shows up. There's no consistent scoring or predictive model behind the checklist.

03 / SILENT ON COST

No financial translation

A list of defects isn't a budget. Buyers are left to guess what repairs actually cost, and when.

How it works

Eight agents, one continuous report.
